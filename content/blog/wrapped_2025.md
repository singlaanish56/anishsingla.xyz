### Music

This was the year that I finally made the shift from Spotify to Apple Music. After a few failed attempts, I was finally able to break a decade long bond with Spotify
It was a combination of multiple things, their poor android app, recommendation algorithm filled with paid or promoted music, and inability to support local or uploaded music properly.

While apple music has its flaws especially on the Android and Windows, two platforms it's not made for. What really pulled me is the beatiful local music sync across apps / platforms and an abosultely beautiful Classical Music App with Listening Guides , histories and perfect recommendations.

Spotify was used for first 3 months, rest is Apple Music
